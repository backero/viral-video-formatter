# Traditional Form Complete Guide & Workflow

## System Architecture Overview

The Traditional Form is a **11-page sequential workflow** that transforms structured creator/content information into a production-ready viral video script. Each page builds on the previous one to define the video's DNA.

### Key Principle: Data Flows → Script Generation

```
Creator Info → Niche Definition → Value Prop → Format Selection 
→ Hook Strategy → Script Output → CTA → Production Details → Approvals
```

---

## PAGE 01: DETAILS - Foundation Layer

**Purpose:** Capture who is making the video, when, and for which company.

**Why it matters:** This grounds the script in reality and enables tracking/accountability.

### Sample Data (Cosmetics Business Example)

| Field | Value | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| **Start Date** | 24-10-2026 | Timeline tracking - when did this project begin? |
| **Creator Name** | Priya Kumar | Who's writing/directing the script? |
| **Creator Dept** | Marketing & Content | Their role determines their perspective on the message |
| **Actor Name** | Anjali Singh | Who will deliver the script on camera? |
| **Actor Dept** | Product & Brand | Product people know the benefits better than random actors |
| **Company/Brand** | BeautyBox Cosmetics | Determines brand voice, values, and product knowledge |

**How it flows forward:** The actor's department (Product & Brand) means Anjali knows the white label vs private label differences deeply → she can deliver the script authentically.

---

## PAGE 02: NICHE/TOPIC - Problem Definition

**Purpose:** Define WHO you're talking to and WHAT problem you're solving.

**Why it matters:** Your hook, script, and CTA must resonate with a specific audience's pain point. Generic content performs poorly.

### Sample Data

| Field | Value | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| **Niche** | Cosmetics & Beauty | Primary category |
| **Sub-Niche** | White Label/Private Label Business | Specific segment = specific audience pain |
| **Topic** | White Label vs Private Label for 2.5 Lakh Budget | The exact problem: someone with that budget wants to know the fastest path to market |
| **Topic Creates** | Demand ✓ Relevance ✓ Interest ✓ Education ✓ | Does this topic naturally create viewer engagement? YES on all four |

**How it flows forward:** 
- **Demand:** Entrepreneurs actually searching "white label cosmetics" monthly = real demand
- **Relevance:** 2.5 lakh budget constraint is a real pain point for this audience
- **Interest:** Comparison naturally hooks people (curiosity about the difference)
- **Education:** Even people who know the difference will watch to confirm they're right

**Script Impact:** The hook will force a COMPARISON (vs generic advice), the value prop will focus on BUDGET EFFICIENCY, the CTA will target ENTREPRENEURS specifically.

---

## PAGE 03: VALUE - The Promise

**Purpose:** What is the MEASURABLE outcome? Not "better marketing" but specific numbers/timelines.

**Why it matters:** Vague value props don't drive action. "Save money" ≠ "Launch in 30 days for ₹2.5L instead of 120 days for ₹5L"

### Sample Data

**Value Proposition (Single Paragraph):**

*"With a 2.5 lakh budget, white label manufacturing is your fastest path to launch. You get proven formulas, low MOQs (100-200 bottles), and can launch 5-6 product variants in weeks instead of months. Private label looks fancy but costs 2x more, requires longer timelines, and has higher risk when your customers don't buy."*

**Measurable Actions (The Real Numbers):**

| Metric | White Label | Private Label | Why This Matters |
|--------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Budget Required** | ₹2.5L | ₹5L+ | Creates urgency: "I can do this NOW" |
| **Timeline** | 30 days | 120+ days | Creates scarcity: faster = competitive advantage |
| **Products/SKUs** | 5-6 variants | 2-3 variants | Creates abundance: more options to test/sell |
| **MOQ (Min Order)** | 100-200 bottles | 1000+ bottles | Creates risk reduction: smaller bets |

**How it flows forward to Script:**
- The script MUST include these 4 metrics
- The hook will emphasize the timeline (fastest path)
- Beat 3 (Budget Breakdown) will show the ₹2.5L split explicitly
- Beat 4 (Social Proof) will mention "30 creators" who made this choice

---

## PAGE 04: FORMAT - Production Scale

**Purpose:** How "produced" should this feel? S-Tier (cinematic) vs F-Tier (raw/authentic)?

**Why it matters:** Format determines budget, crew needs, and viewer expectations.

### Sample Data

| Selection | Meaning | Requirements |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| **S-TIER** | Core Engines (use 70-80%) | High production value, planned shots, color grading, editing |
| *vs B-TIER* | Proof-Heavy (use 5-10%) | Testimonials, case studies, less scripted |
| *vs F-TIER* | Avoid as "structures" | Raw/unpolished (only for authenticity flex) |

**Format Decision → Script Structure Constraint:**

Selecting S-TIER automatically triggers the next page to offer: **Problem-Solution, Storytelling, Step-by-Step, Myth vs Fact** (structured formats).

If you'd selected B-TIER, you'd get: **Comparison, Myth vs Fact** (proof-dependent formats).

---

## PAGE 05: SCRIPT STRUCTURE - Narrative Framework

**Purpose:** HOW will you tell the story? Not WHAT you'll say, but the shape of it.

**Why it matters:** Viewers follow patterns unconsciously. "Problem → Solution" feels more satisfying than random facts.

### Sample Data

| Structure | What It Does | Why For This Topic |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Problem-Solution** | Present obstacle, then overcome it | Perfect for white label (shows the struggle, then relief) |
| *Alternative: Comparison* | Side-by-side contrast | Also works (private label vs white label), but forces different script beats |
| *Alternative: Storytelling* | Character arc (I was stuck, I tried this, I succeeded) | More personal, less "educational" |

**How it flows forward:**

Problem-Solution structure FORCES script beats like:
1. **Beat 1:** Show the problem (private label's high cost/risk)
2. **Beat 2:** Show the solution (white label alternative)
3. **Beat 3:** Prove the solution works (budget breakdown)
4. **Beat 4:** Social proof (30 creators succeeded)
5. **Beat 5:** CTA (act now)

If you'd chosen Storytelling, Beat 1 would be "I was broke and wanted to start a cosmetics brand" (personal) vs "Private label costs 5 lakhs" (factual).

---

## PAGE 06: HOOK - First 3 Seconds

**Purpose:** Stop the scroll. Lock attention before they swipe away.

**Why it matters:** 50% of viewers decide in the first 3 seconds if they'll watch. Hooks are the difference between 100 views and 100K views.

### Sample Data

| Aspect | Choice | What It Does |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| **Hook Type** | Comparison/Contrast | "vs" forces curiosity (what's the difference?) |
| **Visual Hook** | Two bottles side-by-side | Show the "vs" visually |
| **Written Hook** | "I'll show you the choice that made me 15L in 3 months" | Specific result (15L) + specificity (3 months) = credible |
| **Audio Hook** | [VOICE OVER] with urgency | Tone: confident but not salesy |
| **Physiological Factors** | Curiosity, Comparison, Desire, Stakes | Triggered by the "vs" format |

**The Hook in Action:**

Speaker appears with TWO bottles → Audience thinks: "Which is better?"
→ Keeps watching to find out → Stays for full script

**Without a hook:** "Let me tell you about white label cosmetics" → Instant swipe.

---

## PAGE 07: SCRIPT - The Full Story (25 seconds)

**Purpose:** Write the complete dialogue with ACTION markers.

**Why it matters:** This is what the actor will memorize and deliver. Every word counts in 25 seconds.

### Sample Data (Full Script)

```
[ACTION] Speaker sits in front of white background, holds two cosmetic bottles side by side

HOOK (0-3s):
"2.5 lakhs budget for a cosmetics business? 
I'll show you the choice that made me 15 lakhs in 3 months. 
White label. Here's why everyone picks it wrong."

[ACTION] Write on screen: "WHITE LABEL vs PRIVATE LABEL"

BEAT 1 - PROBLEM (3-8s):
"Private label looks better on Instagram, right? 
Custom formula, your name everywhere. 
But you need ₹5 lakhs minimum, 4-5 months waiting time, 
and you're betting your entire budget on ONE formula."

[ACTION] Show red X over private label

BEAT 2 - WHITE LABEL SOLUTION (8-14s):
"White label? 
₹2.5 lakhs gets you 5-6 products ready to sell in 30 days. 
Proven formulas. Low orders. Instant inventory. 
You're not manufacturing — you're reselling."

[ACTION] Show green checkmark over white label

BEAT 3 - BUDGET BREAKDOWN (14-18s):
"Budget split: ₹1 lakh products, ₹50k packaging, ₹1 lakh marketing. 
First month? Sell products, not promises. 
Then upgrade to private label AFTER you make money."

[ACTION] Show calculator/numbers

BEAT 4 - SOCIAL PROOF (18-22s):
"30 creators I've worked with picked white label first. 
Everyone's making 50k+ revenue by month 2. 
Zero failures on timing. Zero surprises on cost."

[ACTION] Show testimonial clips or stat overlay

BEAT 5 - CTA (22-25s):
"Don't overthink this. 
Comment 'COSMETICS' below. 
I'll send you my white label supplier list that everyone's using."

[ACTION] Speaker looks at camera, genuine expression
```

**How It Connects Back:**

- **Hook (page 06):** ✓ Uses the comparison/contrast
- **Structure (page 05):** ✓ Problem → Solution → Proof → Action
- **Value (page 03):** ✓ Includes the 4 key metrics (₹2.5L, 30 days, 5-6 SKUs, MOQ 100-200)
- **Topic (page 02):** ✓ Targets entrepreneurs with budget constraint
- **Format (page 04):** ✓ Includes [ACTION] markers for editing

---

## PAGE 08: CTA - Call to Action

**Purpose:** What specific action should the viewer take right now?

**Why it matters:** "Comment" = algorithm boost. "DM" = direct relationship. "Buy" = revenue.

### Sample Data

```
Comment 'COSMETICS' below. I'll DM you my white label supplier list with MOQs and prices. 
(This is how 40+ entrepreneurs found their manufacturers.)
```

**Design Rules Applied:**

❌ **Banned:** "Click the link in bio" (generic, low-performing)
✅ **Chosen:** "Comment COSMETICS" (specific word, algorithm-friendly, funnel-building)

**How It Flows Back to the Script:**

The CTA is **not invented**—it's baked into Beat 5 of the script. The viewer hears it naturally in context: "Don't overthink this. Comment COSMETICS below."

---

## PAGE 09: PRE-CAMERA / SHOOTING - Production Planning

**Purpose:** Define what the shoot needs before the camera rolls.

**Why it matters:** Bad planning = reshoots = wasted time/budget.

### Sample Data

| Element | Detail | Purpose |
|---------|--------|---------|
| **Ref Video** | "white label cosmetics manufacturing process" (YouTube link) | Actor sees the visual style we're aiming for |
| **Set** | Simple white background, good lighting | Matches the "clean, professional" tone |
| **Props** | 2-3 cosmetic bottles, packaging, white papers | Visualizes the comparison (vs format) |
| **Audio** | Clean voiceover, no background music during explanation | Clarity of message (editing will add music later) |
| **Shooting Duration** | 4-5 hours | Accounts for multiple takes, transitions, setup time |
| **Discussion Date** | 25-10-2026, 2:00 PM IST | Tech team, creator, editor align on vision |

**How It Flows Back:**

The script's [ACTION] markers tell the tech team what's needed:
- "Write on screen: WHITE LABEL vs PRIVATE LABEL" → Need editing software + graphics
- "Show calculator/numbers" → Need motion graphics overlay
- "Show testimonial clips" → Need video clips recorded separately or sourced

---

## PAGE 10: EDITOR - Pre-Shoot Approval

**Purpose:** Confirm shooting can proceed. Approvals from tech/CEO/creator.

**Why it matters:** Prevents "We shot everything, now the CEO hates it" disasters.

### Sample Data

| Approval | Status | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **TECH TEAM** | Approved ✓ | Can we actually make the graphics/animations needed? YES |
| **CEO** | Approved ✓ | Is this message brand-aligned? YES (positioning white label) |
| **CONTENT CREATOR** | Approved ✓ | Do we like the creative direction? YES (comparison format works) |
| **Shoot Date** | 27-10-2026 | 3 days after discussion = enough time to prep |

**Checkpoint Before Shooting:**

This page prevents moving forward if ANY approver says "no." It's a stop-gate.

---

## PAGE 11: FINAL OUT - Post-Production Approval

**Purpose:** After editing, does the final video match what we approved?

**Why it matters:** Edits can subtly change the message (pacing, tone, emphasis). Final check ensures consistency.

### Sample Data

| Approval | Status | Signed By |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **EDITOR** | PENDING | Reviews cut, pacing, color grade, transitions |
| **CEO** | PENDING | Confirms messaging, brand alignment |
| **CONTENT CREATOR** | PENDING | Confirms creative direction feels right |
| **FOUNDER** | PENDING | Final sign-off (only these 3 can say "go live") |

**Founder Approval Status:** PENDING (waits for video link)

## Real-World Example: How This Works in Action

**Scenario:** Priya (Page 01) creates a script about white label cosmetics (Page 02).

**Problem Without This System:**
- Priya writes a generic "start a cosmetics business" script
- Script doesn't mention 2.5 lakh budget (ignores Page 03 value)
- Script is testimonial-heavy (wrong format for Page 04: S-TIER)
- CTA is "DM for details" (vague, low engagement)
- Tech team wastes time rebuilding graphics
- CEO reviews and says "this doesn't feel like our brand"
- Video never ships or ships half-finished

**Solution With This System:**
1. Page 02 forces specific topic (white label + budget) → audience is clear
2. Page 03 locks in 4 metrics → script MUST use these numbers
3. Page 04 (S-TIER) → forces high production, structured beats
4. Page 05 (Problem-Solution) → beats flow naturally
5. Page 06 (Comparison hook) → captures attention in 3s
6. Page 07 fully written before any shoot
7. Page 09 discusses what's actually filmable
8. Page 10 **stops if unapproved** (prevents wasted shoots)
9. Page 11 ensures final video matches approved vision

**Result:** Video ships on time, on-brand, and high-performing because every decision was intentional.

## Key Takeaway

The Traditional Form isn't bureaucracy—it's a **data engine** that ensures every creative decision flows logically from the previous one.

**Each page answers:**
- Page 01: **Who** is making this?
- Page 02: **What** problem are we solving?
- Page 03: **Why** should viewers care? (measurable value)
- Page 04: **How polished** should it be?
- Page 05: **What shape** should the story take?
- Page 06: **How do we stop the scroll?**
- Page 07: **What exactly gets said?**
- Page 08: **What do we ask them to do?**
- Page 09: **What equipment/time do we need?**
- Page 10: **Is this safe to shoot?**
- Page 11: **Does the final product match our vision?**

Change any answer, and everything downstream cascades. That's intentional design.
