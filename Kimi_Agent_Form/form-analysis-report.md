## SECTION 2: CRITICAL GAPS (MISSING COMPONENTS)

### GAP 1: CONTENT INTENT CLASSIFICATION (SOP Section 2.4, 10.0) -- HIGH PRIORITY
**What the SOP says:** Every video must be classified as one of three types:
- **Educational** --> Builds trust (3-5x/week) --> CTA: Follow/Save
- **Storytelling** --> Builds followers (1-2x/week) --> CTA: Follow
- **Authority** --> Generates leads (1x/week) --> CTA: Comment/DM

**What's missing in the form:** No field to select Content Intent / Video Type. This is critical because it determines the CTA type, structure selection, and posting frequency.

**Impact on AI automation:** Without this, the AI cannot determine the correct script structure, CTA type, or validate the weekly content mix.

---

### GAP 2: EDITING / RETENTION ENGINEERING SECTION (SOP Section 8.0, Component 6) -- CRITICAL PRIORITY
**What the SOP says:** Editing is one of the 7 mandatory components. It includes:
- Cuts every <= 2 seconds (ideal 0.8-1.5s)
- Captions mandatory (90% watch muted)
- Micro-retention triggers (visual change, text change, pattern break, object movement every 1-2s)
- Caption readability rules (max 5-7 words/line, max 2 lines, readable in <1 second)
- Font discipline (max 2 fonts, primary for hooks, secondary for captions)
- Visual hierarchy (Face > Hook text > Captions > Background)

**What's missing in the form:** ZERO editing-related fields. This is the biggest single gap in the form. The form jumps from Hook directly to Script with no Editing/Retention planning section.

**Impact on AI automation:** The AI cannot generate editing instructions, caption specs, or retention engineering guidance for Freepik integration.

### GAP 6: THE 7-COMPONENT VALIDATION CHECKLIST (SOP Section 3.2, 3.3.4) -- HIGH PRIORITY
**What the SOP says:** Before filming, ALL 7 components must be consciously decided:
1. Topic - Does it already attract attention?
2. Hook - Would a stranger stop scrolling?
3. Value - Can the viewer apply something immediately?
4. Structure - Does viewer feel compelled to watch till end?
5. Format - Is this format proven on this account?
6. Editing - Is attention refreshed every 1-2 seconds?
7. CTA - Does viewer know exactly what to do?

Plus the One-Line Validation Check:
- Does frame-1 interrupt? YES
- Is the promise obvious? YES
- Does value arrive fast? YES
- Is value visually shown? YES
- Is there only ONE CTA? YES

**What's missing in the form:** No pre-filming validation checklist. No way to confirm all 7 components have been addressed. No pass/fail mechanism.
