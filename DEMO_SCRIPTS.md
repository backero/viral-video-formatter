# 🎬 QUICK REFERENCE: Hook Types Used

✅ **Tutorial Hook** (Script 1)
- Best for: How-tos, processes, step-by-step
- Opening: "Here's something most people don't know..."

✅ **Transformation Hook** (Script 2)
- Best for: Results, changes, before & after
- Opening: "I went from X to Y..."

✅ **Comparison Hook** (Scripts 3, 5)
- Best for: Price, quality, alternatives
- Opening: "Most people think X, but actually Y..."

✅ **Curiosity Gap Hook** (Script 4)
- Best for: Secrets, hacks, exclusive info
- Opening: "This one thing is worth $100k..."

✅ **Shock/Contradiction Hook** (Script 5)
- Best for: Surprising facts, myths
- Opening: "Everyone believes X, but it's actually Y..."

✅ **Outcome-First Hook** (Script 6)
- Best for: Results, gains, transformations
- Opening: "I made $5000 in 6 months..."
